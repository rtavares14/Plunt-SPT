# myPlunt 

> A plant social app for you and your friends. Because plants thrive with company.

**Status: Pre-launch.** Join the waitlist at [myplunt.com](https://myplunt.com).

---

## What is myPlunt?

myPlunt was born from a simple realization: **most plant apps are either too cold or too public.**

They are either clinical spreadsheets ("Water in 3 days") or social-media-like apps ("Like my Monstera"). myPlunt sits in the middle. It provides the high-fidelity tools you need to keep your plants alive, while creating a private space to share that experience with the people who actually care.

### The Three Pillars of myPlunt

 #### 1. It's realistic
 It’s a digital greenhouse where you can be honest about your plant's progress—whether it's thriving or struggling—without the pressure of social media aesthetics.

#### 2. Environmental Intelligence
Plants are deeply affected by their surroundings. myPlunt ties each plant to a real-world city location, allowing the app to factor in local weather patterns. 
- **Frost Alerts:** Get notified when a cold snap threatens your outdoor planters.
- **Heatwave Nudges:** See that a friend's city is hitting 40°C? Send them a quick reminder to check their soil.
- **Threshold Monitoring:** Track temperature and humidity against your plant's specific needs.

#### 3. Collaborative Care 
Plant care is better with friends. myPlunt lets you peek into your friends' gardens, swap advice, remind each other to water or move plants and keep each other accountable. It’s designed to feel more like a supportive group than a competitive network.
